Rules

29 November 2026 · IST

Schedule

  • Date: Sunday, 29 November 2026 (all times in IST)
  • 11:00 AM – 04:00 PM: Solve the challenges and capture flags.
  • 04:00 PM – 06:00 PM: Write your solution document: explain how you solved each challenge.
  • Flag submission closes automatically at 04:00 PM. The countdown on this site runs on the server clock, so it is the official time.

Eligibility and accounts

  1. Each person may register exactly one account. Duplicate or shared accounts will be disqualified.
  2. Use accurate details when you register. Usernames must not be offensive.

Solving challenges

  1. Flags use the format ZERODAY{...} unless a challenge says otherwise. Submit the full flag, including the braces.
  2. Every challenge has a point value. Some challenges use dynamic scoring, where the value drops as more participants solve them.
  3. Hints may cost points. The cost is shown before you unlock a hint.
  4. Rapid repeated guessing is rate-limited. Automated flag brute-forcing is prohibited.
  5. Ranking is by total points. Ties are broken in favour of the participant who reached that score first.

Solution document (04:00 PM – 06:00 PM)

Mandatory for every participant who solved at least one challenge. You must explain how you solved each challenge you submitted.
  1. After solving ends at 04:00 PM, log in and open the Writeup page. It lists every challenge you solved.
  2. For each challenge, write the steps you followed: what you noticed, which tools or techniques you used, and how you reached the flag.
  3. Upload screenshots that show your work (PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP, up to 8 MB each, up to 10 per challenge).
  4. A challenge counts as explained only when it has written steps (at least 50 characters) and at least one screenshot. Press Save writeup on each challenge.
  5. You can edit until 06:00 PM. After that the Writeup page is locked and nothing more is accepted.
  6. Organizers review these writeups to verify solves. Solves that cannot be explained, or writeups that are copied or fabricated, may be removed from the ranking or lead to disqualification.

Fair play

  1. Work alone. Do not collaborate with, or receive help from, other people during the event.
  2. Do not share flags, hints, solutions or writeups with anyone, publicly or privately, until the event has ended.
  3. Do not attack, scan, overload or attempt to break the ZeroDayCTF platform, its servers, or other participants. Only target the challenge labs and assets provided for each challenge.
  4. Denial-of-service, destructive actions and attempts to access other participants' data or accounts are strictly prohibited.
  5. If you find a bug in the platform or in a challenge, report it to the organizers. Do not exploit it for points.

Disqualification

Breaking any of these rules can lead to a warning, removal of points, or disqualification, at the organizers' sole discretion. Organizers' decisions are final.

Good luck, hacker! Hack. Learn. Compete.
